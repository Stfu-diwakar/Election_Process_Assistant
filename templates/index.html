WHY THIS PROJECT EXISTS

Making civic knowledge easier to reach.

Election Process Assistant is an interactive civic-education project designed to make the stages of an election easier to understand.

It brings together a guided election timeline and an AI-powered question-and-answer experience. Explore the process at your own pace, revisit a stage whenever you need, and ask questions in plain language.
